OTC Cafe

The garden was built around the tree, not the other way round.

Most places on this stretch of NH‑44 cleared the plot first and decided what to plant afterwards. OTC did the opposite. The tables were laid out to keep the canopy overhead, which is why the seating wanders instead of sitting in rows, and why the light on your table moves through the afternoon.

It means a few things in practice. The garden is cooler than the car park by a noticeable margin. Nobody minds a dog under the table. And at about seven in the evening, when the lights come on in the branches, the place stops looking like a cafe on a highway.

The plant-covered seating area is the reason most people come back — you forget the highway is right there.

What you get when you sit down

  • Open garden seating

    Tables under a plant canopy, and an air-conditioned room inside for the months when that stops being charming.

  • Dogs are welcome

    Bring them. The garden side is the easy place to sit with a pet, and nobody will look at you strangely.

  • Live music nights

    Acoustic sets under the tree. Ring ahead to find out who is playing this week.

  • Match nights on screen

    The big games are screened. Come early on a match day — the garden fills before the toss.

  • Kids are fine here

    Room to move around, food that isn't only for adults, and no glassware within reach of a toddler.

  • Takeaway & delivery

    The full kitchen packs to go. Call the cafe directly and skip the delivery-app markup.

No alcohol served. Air-conditioned indoors. Roadside parking on the service lane.

Before you come

Can we bring a dog?

Yes, in the garden. Water bowls on request. The indoor room is kept pet-free for people who would rather it was.

Do we need to book?

Not on a weekday afternoon. For Friday and Saturday evenings, and any match night, book — the garden tables go first.

Is there parking?

Roadside on the service lane. It fills up on weekend evenings, so leave a few minutes for it.

Do you serve alcohol?

No. The bar is coffee, tea, coolers and shakes.

Can you do a large group or a birthday?

Yes — call ahead so a section of the garden can be held. Outside cake is fine.

What is the last order?

The kitchen takes its last order at 22:45. The cafe closes at 23:00.
